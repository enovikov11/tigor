## What it does

Jupyter notebook analyzing the EFF large wordlist for passphrase generation. Computes the distribution of word lengths (7, 8, 9 characters), then calculates all possible total-length combinations when selecting 4 words. This helps understand the entropy characteristics of EFF wordlist–based passphrases and plan for fixed-width passphrase storage formats.

- `main.ipynb` — Notebook that loads `eff_large_wordlist.txt`, computes word-length distribution, and enumerates all 4-word combination total lengths with probability weights. Also experiments with a curated subset (1591 words of length 7, 1779 of length 8, 1557 of length 9).
